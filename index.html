About SNEK

Classic Snake with modern polish. Canvas-rendered, responsive, keyboard + touch + swipe support, local high-score persistence, and adjustable grid/speed.

  • Apples: Red (10 pts), Gold (50 pts, 10% spawn chance)
  • Walls: Solid ends game; Wrap teleports you.
  • Scoring: Score increases speed every 50 pts.
